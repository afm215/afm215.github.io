## Overview

**Diff-CA** introduces a generative contrastive analysis framework based on continuous flow-matching and diffusion models to separate **common factors** shared between a background and target domain from **salient factors** specific to the target distribution, enabling principled factor disentanglement and counterfactual visual synthesis.
